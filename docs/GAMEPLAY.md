# Landor — Gameplay

*A tiny game for big ideas.* This guide is for players. No computer talk here.

## The valley

There is a little valley up in the hills. It is your home. You can walk anywhere
you like.

But the valley holds secrets! Shiny **shards** are hidden all over the place. And
deep inside, behind a locked gate, there is a **beacon**.

**Your job:** collect all three shards, then reach the beacon with them. That is how you win. 🎉

## How to walk around

| Press | You...                    |
| ----- | ------------------------- |
| `W`   | go up (north)             |
| `S`   | go down (south)           |
| `A`   | go left (west)            |
| `D`   | go right (east)           |

Arrow keys work too. Press `E` to look at things and use them. Press `Q` when you
want to stop playing.

You can walk right onto things you can pick up, and they are yours. For big things
like chests, gates, doors and signposts, stand next to them and press `E`.

## What the pictures mean

You play with little pictures made of typing characters. Here they are:

| Picture | What it is                                       |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| `@`     | **You!**                                         |
| `.`     | grass — nice to walk on                          |
| `,`     | dirt path — the way people walk                  |
| `~`     | water — you will get wet, so don't               |
| `=`     | bridge — walk over the water, clever!            |
| `#`     | rock — too hard to squeeze past                  |
| `T`     | tree — bump! no way through                      |
| `H`     | house wall                                       |
| `d`/`D` | door that is shut / door that is open            |
| `C`/`c` | chest still full / chest already opened          |
| `*`     | signpost — press `E` to read it                  |
| `G`     | gate — needs a key to open                       |
| `K`     | a **key**                                        |
| `S`     | a **shard** — one of the hidden treasure pieces  |
| `B`     | the **beacon** — walk here with all three shards to win   |

## Good things to know

* **You can only see so far.** Everything past that is dark, like fog. When you
  walk away from a place it goes dim, but you still remember it. Watch the top of
  the screen: it tells you how much of the valley you have found so far.
* **Night comes.** After a while the day turns into night, and then you cannot see
  nearly as far. Stick to paths you already know!
* **Three shards await across the valley.** Collect them all to win.
* **The key is in a chest.** Chests do not open by walking into them — stand next
  to one and press `E`.
* **The gate is the only way in** to the room with the beacon. Keys open gates. One
  key opens one gate.
* Signs will point you the right way if you get lost. Reading signs is never a
  waste of time.

## The neighbours

Later on, the valley gets busier. There will be people, animals and monsters
living there. They go about their own business: some look for things, some help,
some try to smash what they cannot open, and some are just plain nasty. They will
talk to you, and you can talk back. What they decide to do depends on what kind of
creature they are — good ones ask nicely, and unkind ones take what they want.

Some of them carry tools. Think carefully about who you ask for help.

## Changing the world

You can also leave your mark on the valley itself: dig, break stuff, make a path
where there was none. If you do something mean — like breaking through somebody's
wall — the valley remembers who did it. Best not to blame your friend for it.

## Getting stuck?

Walk back along where you came from, or try another way around. If you dig
yourself into a spot with no way out, do not worry: the game will not let you dig
the one wall that would trap you. And if you still get stuck, starting a new game
is always fine — nothing bad happens, you just try again.

Have fun — the valley is yours.
